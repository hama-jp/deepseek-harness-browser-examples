Formulas & shortcuts

Formulas

Start any formula with =. Reference cells like A1, or use $A$1 for an absolute reference when copying.

  • =SUM(B2:B9)Add a range
  • =AVERAGE(B2:B9)Average values
  • =(B2-B3)*12Arithmetic
  • =IF(B2>1000,"Yes","No")Conditional
  • =ROUND(B2/3,2)Round a result
  • =MAX(B2:B9)-MIN(B2:B9)Range functions

Also supports COUNT, COUNTA, PRODUCT, ABS, IFERROR, AND, OR, NOT, &, and comparisons.

While editing a formula, Point mode lets you click a cell or use the arrow keys to insert its reference. Drag across cells, or hold Shift while pressing arrows, to pick a range such as A1:B3. Press F2 to switch between Point and Caret mode.
